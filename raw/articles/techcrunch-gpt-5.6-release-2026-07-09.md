# OpenAI launches its new family of models with GPT-5.6

By Lucas Ropek, TechCrunch — July 9, 2026

OpenAI unveiled its newest family of models on Thursday, July 9, 2026, introducing GPT-5.6 in three variants:

- **Sol** — Flagship / workhorse, best coding model yet
- **Terra** — Balanced everyday model, GPT-5.5-competitive at 2x lower cost
- **Luna** — Fastest and most cost-efficient

## Performance Highlights

- **Coding Agent Index**: Sol scores 80, 2.8 points above Anthropic Fable 5, using less than half the output tokens, half the time, and costing ~1/3 less
- **Terra** performs just above Fable 5; **Luna** outperforms Opus 4.8
- **Token efficiency**: Sam Altman told CNBC Sol is 54% more token efficient on agentic coding tasks vs Fable
- **Cybersecurity**: Called "strongest cybersecurity model yet" — supports threat modeling, code review, patching, and blue teaming
- **Agents' Last Exam**: Sol scores 53.6, beating Fable 5 (adaptive reasoning) by 13.1 points

## US Government Intervention

The Trump administration requested OpenAI delay the rollout due to national security concerns over potential misuse. OpenAI made "many changes" after "collaborative back and forth" with the administration. This follows a similar pattern to Anthropic's Fable 5/Mythos 5 export control order in June.

## Pricing (per 1M tokens)

| Model | Input | Output |
|-------|-------|--------|
| Sol   | $5    | $30    |
| Terra | $2.50 | $15    |
| Luna  | $1    | $6     |

## ChatGPT Work

Alongside the models, OpenAI launched ChatGPT Work — an AI agent for enterprise teams that can gather context across connected apps and files to create documents, spreadsheets, presentations, and other work. Available on Mac, Windows, web, and mobile.

## Competitive Context

This release comes the same week as new models from SpaceXAI (Grok) and Meta (Muse Spark 1.1). Early reviews are mixed: many praise GPT-5.6's reliability and efficiency, while some testers still prefer Anthropic Fable 5 for raw intelligence on certain tasks.
