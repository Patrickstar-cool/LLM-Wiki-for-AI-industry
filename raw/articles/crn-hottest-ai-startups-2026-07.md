# The 10 Hottest AI Startups of 2026 (So Far)

Published: July 2026 | Source: CRN

## Investment Landscape
- $300 billion poured into 6,000 startups globally in Q1 2026
- 80% of total global venture funding went to AI startups
- Most investment went to US-based AI startups

## Key Startups

### Anthropic
- CEO: Dario Amodei
- Raised $65 billion in Series H (May 2026), valuation $965 billion
- Annual run rate revenue crossed $47 billion
- Acquired Stainless (May), Coefficient Bio (April), Vecept (February), Bun (December)
- Claude available on AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure

### Cognition AI
- CEO: Scott Wu
- Maker of Devin, autonomous AI software engineer
- Raised $1 billion, valuation $26 billion (May 2026)
- 90% of Cognition's internal code now written by Devin
- Partnership with Carahsoft for public sector (June 2026)

### Cohere
- CEO: Aidan Gomez
- Acquired/merged with Aleph Alpha (Germany), valuation ~$20 billion (April 2026)
- Acquired Reliant AI (May 2026) for sovereign AI strategy
- Launched North Mini Code, first agentic open-source coding model (June 2026)
- Positioning as sovereign AI alternative for regulated industries

### Command Zero
- CEO: Dov Yoran
- Autonomous & AI-assisted SOC platform
- Released MCP server for LLM-based agents (April 2026)

### Helix Digital
- CEO: Adam Selipsky (former AWS CEO)
- $20 billion in capital
- Founded by KKR, Nvidia, Vistra, Kuwait Investment Authority
- Building next-generation AI infrastructure: data centers + power + connectivity

### Mistral AI
- European AI leader
- Raised $830M for Nvidia data center with 13,800 GPUs

### Prometheus
- Founded by Jeff Bezos
- $12 billion funding round, valuation $41 billion (June 2026)
- Building AI models for physical tasks: engineering, manufacturing, drug design
- Hired top researchers from OpenAI and DeepMind

### Together AI
- CEO: Vipul Ved Prakash
- AI cloud platform: serverless inference, fine-tuning for open-source AI
- Powers over 1 million developers
- Multi-year agreement with Rumble for Nvidia HGX B300 GPU capacity

### Writer
- CEO: May Habib
- Enterprise AI agent platform with Palmyra LLMs
- Knowledge Graph + RAG for secure enterprise deployment
- Appointed first COO and CCO in 2026
