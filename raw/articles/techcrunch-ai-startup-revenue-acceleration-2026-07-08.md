# These AI startups are growing revenue at faster and faster rates

**Source:** TechCrunch
**Author:** Marina Temkin
**Published:** July 8, 2026

## Overview

Multiple AI startups report revenue growth that is not just growing, but *accelerating* — hitting each subsequent milestone in shorter timeframes. Note: companies use different metrics (ARR, annualized run-rate, committed ARR, trailing 12-month revenue).

## Revenue Acceleration Data

### Mercor
- $2B gross annualized revenue (June 2026) — just 4 months after $1B milestone
- $500M run rate (Sept 2025)
- Founded: <3 years ago
- Business: Hires domain experts to train/refine AI models

### Anthropic
- **$47B revenue run rate** (May 2026) — less than 2 months after $30B
- $30B (late Mar 2026)
- $9B (late 2025)
- $4B (July 2025)
- **Growth: ~12x in 10 months**

### Sierra
- $200M ARR (May 2026) — just 2 quarters after reaching $100M
- $100M ARR took 7 quarters
- Founded by Bret Taylor (ex-Salesforce co-CEO)
- Business: Customer service AI agents for enterprises

### Glean
- $300M ARR (May 2026) — 6 months from $200M
- $200M ARR took 9 months from $100M
- Founded: 7 years
- Business: Enterprise AI search/knowledge management

### Gusto
- $1B trailing 12-month revenue (May 2026)
- Revenue accelerated each of last 5 quarters
- 14-year-old HR tech company — shows AI benefits aren't limited to AI-native startups
- Last valued: $9.3B (2022)

### Clio
- $500M ARR (May 2026) — doubled from $200M in ~6 months
- Embedded AI in 2023 — revenue took off sharply
- 18-year-old legal practice management software

## Key Takeaway

AI is not just creating new startups — it's supercharging existing SaaS companies. The "flywheel effect" is real: as AI capabilities improve, customers adopt faster, revenue compounds, funding follows, enabling more AI investment.
