# AI price wars: OpenAI cuts GPT-5.6 Luna prices by 80% as model competition shifts toward cost

**Source:** VentureBeat
**Published:** July 30, 2026

---

## Summary

OpenAI sharply reduced API prices for GPT-5.6 series on July 30, 2026:
- **GPT-5.6 Luna**: 80% cut — now $0.20/$1.20 per 1M input/output tokens (was $1/$6)
- **GPT-5.6 Terra**: 20% cut — now $2/$12 per 1M tokens (was $2.50/$15)
- **GPT-5.6 Sol**: New "Fast mode" — 2.5x throughput at 2x price ($10/$60 per 1M tokens)

Combined pricing:
- Luna: $1.40/1M (from $7) — now competitive with low-cost tier
- Terra: $14/1M (from $17.50) — matches Google Gemini 3.1 Pro pricing
- Sol Standard: $35/1M (unchanged)
- Sol Fast: $70/1M (new premium tier)

## Key Context

- Cuts come after Anthropic released Claude Opus 5 at Opus 4.8 pricing, and Google introduced Gemini 3.6 Flash/3.5 Flash-Lite
- Per Artificial Analysis, even Luna outperforms Gemini 3.6 Flash on intelligence index
- Efficiency gains partly from Sol autonomously rewriting production code kernels, cutting serving costs by 20%
- Cognition (Devin) noted GPT-5.6 now "sits on the pareto curve of price/performance efficiency"

## Market Implications

- Luna moves from middle-market to near-lowest cost tier
- Three-way competitive dynamic: OpenAI cuts per-token rates, Google pairs lower price with reduced token usage, Anthropic emphasizes task performance at unchanged price
- Market shift from "model access" to "model economics" — cost of completing production work
