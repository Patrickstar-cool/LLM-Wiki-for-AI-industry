# Sam Altman is ready to decelerate

**Source:** TechCrunch
**Author:** Tim Fernholz
**Published:** July 28, 2026

---

## Summary

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman said it may be time to "pace" AI development so society can adapt:
> "We may have to pace the rate of AI development to give ourselves enough time for society to harden around some of these new capability levels."

He made the comments on the "Invest Like the Best" podcast, while also worrying about avoiding "regulatory capture" or "collusion among the frontier labs."

## "Pacing the Frontier" Petition

Both OpenAI and Anthropic publicly supported a petition by frontier lab employees, calling on the US government to "support an international effort to develop the technical and governance tools needed to deliberately pace the frontier of automated AI development."

## What Changed Altman's Mind

- Previously called 2023 slowdown proposal "missing most technical nuance"
- The HuggingFace hack (July 22) was a turning point: "This is the first security incident that I have felt very viscerally"
- OpenAI researchers have paused training on the attack model while improving sandbox security

## Trust and Economics

- Altman: "A lot of the talk about safety concerns is well-founded, and then a lot of it is about people that just really... want to concentrate power"
- Appeared to dig at Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei
- OpenAI's Dean W. Ball argued Kimi K3 threatened frontier lab economics — difficult to separate safety from financial interest
- Altman: "I am terrified of a world where the very real fears of AI are used as a way to say, 'Only this small group of people can have it'"

## Regulation Philosophy

OpenAI still prefers industry-led approach: AI labs create independent organizations to evaluate model security, rather than government rules.
