# Prentis, new AI lab co-founded by Reid Hoffman, Mark Pincus in talks to raise $100M

**Source:** TechCrunch
**Author:** Marina Temkin
**Published:** July 24, 2026

---

## Summary

**Prentis**, a new AI research lab focused on computer-use models, is in talks to raise **$100M at $1B valuation**. Co-founded by:
- **Ritankar Das** (CEO) — UC Berkeley's youngest University Medalist, founder of Titan holding company
- **Reid Hoffman** — LinkedIn co-founder, Greylock partner, early OpenAI investor
- **Mark Pincus** — Zynga founder, runs Reinvent Capital

## Technology

Trains models to learn office worker workflows, building AI agents that control computers to automate tasks.
- **Hive-32B** model claims to outperform GPT-5.4 and Claude Opus 4.6 on WindowsAgentArena and ScreenSpot-v2
- Claims ~10x lower cost per task than frontier APIs
- Signed contracts worth up to $50M (healthcare, manufacturing, goods companies)

## Business

- Projects $75M annualized run rate by Q3 2026
- Revenue: 20% of savings realized (performance-dependent)
- Use cases: insurance claims, customs duty refund automation

## Team

25+ employees from OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Meta, Tencent, Alibaba.

## Competitive Context

Anthropic, OpenAI, and Thinking Machines Lab (Mira Murati) also developing computer-use agents. Anthropic acquired Vercept (Seattle computer-use startup) in early 2026.
