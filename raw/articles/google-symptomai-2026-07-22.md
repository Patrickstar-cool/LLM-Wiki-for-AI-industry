# SymptomAI: Towards a conversational AI agent for everyday symptom assessment

Published: July 22, 2026 | Authors: Joseph Breda, Jake Sunshine (Google Research)
Paper: arXiv:2605.04012

Google Research presents a first-of-its-kind research study of AI for differential diagnosis and symptom checking through a national-scale study.

## Study Design
- 13,917 consenting research participants
- 5 randomized SymptomAI agents powered by Gemini Flash 2.0
- Each with varying degrees of flexibility in conducting symptom interviews
- Participants described symptoms, SymptomAI asked follow-up questions, culminating in a differential diagnosis (DDx)
- Two weeks later, participants reported diagnoses received from healthcare providers
- Clinical expert annotation study: 3 board-certified clinicians reviewed transcripts and ranked DDx quality

## Key Results

### Clinical experts preferred SymptomAI DDx
Clinicians preferred the DDx generated by SymptomAI over those provided by other clinicians in over 50% of cases.

### SymptomAI DDx more accurate
SymptomAI's DDx had higher top-5 accuracy (containing the true diagnosis from the participant's healthcare provider) than DDx from other clinicians.

### Agent-driven prompting outperforms base LLM
All agent-driven prompting strategies (where SymptomAI actively asked follow-up questions) significantly outperformed the Base condition (fully user-driven, like current chatbot experience).

### Performance on low-confidence cases
SymptomAI's performance above clinical baselines was greatest for cases where clinicians felt least confident in their own DDx.

### Biosignal correlation
SymptomAI diagnoses correlated with wearable biosignal shifts (Fitbit data): respiratory infection cohorts showed clear physiological changes in days leading up to symptom reporting — including cardiovascular, respiration, skin temperature, and sleep quality changes.

## Limitations
- Differential diagnosis is inherently ambiguous; diagnoses may develop longitudinally
- Clinicians reviewed static transcripts without agency to ask follow-up questions
- Cannot replace body language, visual assessment, medical records, or existing patient rapport
- All diagnoses are AI-derived for research analysis only, not confirmed clinical diagnoses
